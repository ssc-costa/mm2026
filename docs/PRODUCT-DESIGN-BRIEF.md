# Mr Move Website Product & Design Brief

**Status:** Agreed working brief  
**Repository:** `ssc-costa/mm2026`  
**Working branch:** `dev` only until explicit production sign-off  
**Primary market:** Costa Blanca, Spain, with UK–Spain and Spain–UK relocation services  
**Primary objective:** Increase qualified quote enquiries without weakening trust, performance, or the live site

---

## 1. Project Goal

Build the strongest removals website in the Costa Blanca market by making Mr Move feel clearer, more credible, more active, and easier to contact than competitors.

The website should operate as a sales tool rather than a static brochure. Every major section should either:

1. Help a visitor understand which service they need.
2. Build trust in Mr Move.
3. Move the visitor towards a quote, phone call, or WhatsApp conversation.

The site should feel professional, experienced, direct, friendly, and locally grounded. It should not feel corporate, generic, over-designed, or dependent on stock photography.

---

## 2. Non-Negotiable Constraints

- The current live website must not be broken or edited directly.
- All development work must remain on a development branch until Kev explicitly signs off.
- Mobile-first design is essential.
- No code should be promoted to production without review and approval.
- Real company photography should be used wherever possible.
- Existing images may be used as placeholders until a stronger image library is available.
- No public pricing estimator or indicative pricing tool is included in the current scope.
- The homepage hero must contain an immediately accessible quote form.
- Product and design decisions should be challenged where a stronger alternative exists.

---

## 3. Primary Business Pillars

The business should be presented through four equally important service pillars.

### 3.1 Local Removals

General household, apartment, office, man-and-van, and full-crew removals across the Costa Blanca.

Key messages:

- Local experience
- Reliable team
- Insured handling
- Flexible support for small and large moves
- Knowledge of apartment access, local roads, urbanisations, and difficult properties

### 3.2 UK–Spain and Spain–UK Removals

International relocation services in both directions.

Key messages:

- Spain-to-UK and UK-to-Spain routes
- Full and part-load moves where offered
- Planning and communication
- Customs documentation support where applicable
- Secure handling throughout the journey

This is a major revenue and differentiation area and should receive strong homepage visibility and its own dedicated landing page.

### 3.3 Secure Storage in Catral

Storage must be positioned as a serious core service, not a minor add-on.

Key messages:

- Secure storage facility in Catral
- Short-term and long-term options
- Collection and redelivery support
- Suitable for moves, renovations, delayed completions, downsizing, seasonal storage, and international relocations
- Real images of the facility should be prioritised when available

The site should clearly explain how storage works and remove uncertainty around access, collection, security, insurance, and redelivery.

### 3.4 Crane and Specialist Lifting

Crane-assisted moves should be treated as a major differentiator.

Key messages:

- Sofas, jacuzzis, furniture, and bulky items
- Apartment balconies and upper floors
- Narrow stairs or inaccessible internal routes
- Roof terraces and difficult properties
- Planned, safe, specialist handling

This service should use real job photography and short case-study style examples because the visual proof is more persuasive than generic claims.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Local movers

People moving between homes, apartments, urbanisations, or offices on the Costa Blanca.

### International movers

People relocating between Spain and the UK who need reassurance, communication, planning, and safe transport.

### Storage customers

People needing temporary or longer-term storage during moving, renovation, travel, downsizing, delayed property completion, or life changes.

### Specialist-access customers

People with large or awkward items that cannot be moved through conventional access routes.

### Mobile-first users

A large proportion of visitors are expected to arrive from Google, Facebook, referrals, or WhatsApp on a mobile device. They may be stressed, comparing several companies, and looking for immediate reassurance and contact.

---

## 5. Core User Questions

The homepage should answer these within seconds:

1. Who is Mr Move?
2. What services are offered?
3. Where does the company operate?
4. Can the company handle my particular move?
5. Why should I trust them?
6. How quickly can I request a quote?
7. Can I contact them through phone or WhatsApp instead?

---

## 6. Conversion Strategy

### Primary conversion

A completed quote request.

### Secondary conversions

- WhatsApp enquiry
- Phone call
- Click through to a dedicated service page
- Google review interaction
- Facebook post interaction

### Hero quote form

The homepage hero must include a short, low-friction quote-start form.

Recommended first-step fields:

- Moving from
- Moving to
- Approximate move date
- Phone or WhatsApp number
- Continue / Get My Quote

The first step should not ask for every possible detail. A long form inside the hero would create unnecessary friction, particularly on mobile.

### Quote flow

Use a progressive two-stage journey:

**Stage one:** Fast enquiry start in the hero.  
**Stage two:** Property details, storage needs, packing, crane/access requirements, message, and optional photographs.

The system should clearly explain what happens after submission, including expected response timing.

### Contact alternatives

Phone and WhatsApp should remain first-class actions, not buried fallback options.

## 8. Dedicated Landing Pages

Each core pillar should have a dedicated page rather than relying only on homepage sections.

Recommended initial pages:

- `/local-removals/`
- `/uk-spain-removals/`
- `/storage-catral/`
- `/crane-lifting/`

Each page should include:

- Service-specific headline
- Clear explanation of the service
- Real images
- Relevant testimonials
- Common use cases
- Process
- FAQs
- Strong quote action
- Phone and WhatsApp alternatives

The homepage acts as the switchboard. Dedicated pages do the deeper selling and search optimisation.

---

## 9. Trust Strategy

Trust must be demonstrated, not merely claimed.

Priority proof elements:

- Google rating and review count
- Selected genuine Google reviews
- Reviews matched to relevant services where possible
- Real team photographs
- Real fleet photographs
- Storage facility photographs
- Crane-job photographs
- Recent completed moves
- Insurance and registration details where appropriate
- Clear contact details
- Realistic response expectations
- Consistent branding across website, Facebook, Google, email, and vehicles

Anonymous testimonials should be replaced by named or verifiable reviews where permission and platform rules allow.

---

## 10. Social Media Strategy

Facebook is an active content source and should help keep the website fresh.

Do not use a raw Facebook page-feed embed as the default approach because it can be slow, difficult to style, unreliable, and distracting.

Preferred approach:

### Latest Moves section

- Display three to six selected recent posts
- Use real job images
- Show a short caption and date or location
- Link to the original Facebook post
- Match the visual design of the website
- Cache content so the website does not depend on Facebook loading in real time
- Hide or fall back gracefully if Facebook access fails

Technical feasibility depends on Meta permissions and token maintenance. If fully automated access is unreliable, use a lightweight approval or import workflow rather than allowing Facebook to control homepage reliability.

## 11. Visual Design Direction

### Brand qualities

- Professional
- Confident
- Experienced
- Practical
- Friendly
- Local
- Honest
- Strong without feeling aggressive

### Colour palette

The current company palette should remain the base:

- Black / near-black backgrounds
- White primary text
- Red for primary actions and brand energy
- Gold for trust, premium accents, and highlights

The palette must be formalised into consistent design tokens before implementation. Red and gold should be used deliberately rather than competing everywhere for attention.

### Typography

- Highly legible on mobile
- Strong, confident headings
- Comfortable body text
- Minimum 16px form input text on mobile
- Clear hierarchy
- Avoid excessive all-caps body content

### Buttons

- One visually dominant primary action per page
- Large mobile touch targets
- Consistent shapes and states
- Clear labels such as `Get a Quote`, `Continue`, `Call Mr Move`, or `WhatsApp Us`

### Imagery rules

- Real Mr Move photography is preferred over stock imagery
- Real jobs should be prioritised even when images are less polished
- Avoid generic removal-stock imagery unless no suitable alternative exists
- Images should show people, vans, storage, lifts, challenging access, and completed work
- Existing available images may be used as placeholders
- Images should be compressed, locally controlled, and supplied with useful alt text

The guiding principle is **authenticity over artificial polish**, while still presenting images cleanly and professionally.

---

## 12. Content Tone

The copy should be:

- Plain English
- Direct
- Reassuring
- Experienced
- Warm without being over-friendly
- Occasionally witty where appropriate
- Free from empty marketing language

The existing personality in the copy is worth retaining. It should not become bland corporate removals language.

Humour must never undermine reassurance, professionalism, safety, or trust.

---

## 13. Mobile-First Requirements

- Quote form accessible without scrolling through the entire homepage
- Large tap targets
- Phone and WhatsApp easy to reach
- No horizontal overflow
- No oversized navigation
- Forms should use appropriate mobile input types
- Inputs must use at least 16px text to prevent browser zoom
- Bottom sticky actions must respect device safe areas
- Images must be responsive and optimised
- Content hierarchy must remain clear on narrow screens
- Essential content must not depend on hover states
- Performance should be tested on average mobile connections, not only desktop broadband

---

## 14. Accessibility Requirements

- Semantic headings
- Visible keyboard focus states
- Sufficient colour contrast
- Accessible form labels and error messages
- Clear validation
- Mobile navigation focus handling
- Accordion state communicated correctly
- Meaningful image alt text
- Reduced-motion consideration for animation
- No essential information conveyed through colour alone

---

## 15. Technical Direction

The current site is a lightweight static website with a serverless contact endpoint. A framework migration should not be performed merely for fashion or developer preference.

Recommended near-term approach:

- Preserve the lightweight deployment model
- Separate CSS, JavaScript, translations, and content where practical
- Store important brand images within controlled project assets
- Improve form reliability and validation
- Add analytics events for quote starts, completions, phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks, and service-page visits
- Keep production isolated from development work

A lightweight static framework such as Astro may be considered when the site expands into multiple service pages, location pages, structured case studies, or bilingual content. This decision should be based on maintainability and content requirements rather than novelty.

---

## 16. Quote Form Technical Requirements

- Server-side validation for required fields
- Spam protection beyond a basic honeypot where necessary
- Rate limiting or equivalent abuse protection
- Clear success and failure states
- Customer confirmation message or email
- Reliable internal notification
- Correct privacy wording
- Consent and privacy-policy link
- No misleading reference to a form provider not actually in use
- Lead persistence or backup strategy so enquiries do not silently disappear
- Tracking of quote start and quote completion
- Sensitive data minimisation

---

## 17. SEO Direction

The homepage should not attempt to rank for every service and location by becoming endlessly long.

Initial SEO priorities:

- Strong homepage focused on Mr Move and Costa Blanca removals
- Dedicated pages for the four pillars
- Clear titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, and structured data
- Genuine service-specific FAQs
- Useful original content and real job examples
- Proper bilingual URL structure if Spanish content is expanded
- Carefully selected location pages only where genuinely unique content can be provided

Avoid producing many nearly identical town pages. Search growth should come from useful, specific content rather than geographic copy duplication.

---

## 21. Working Principles

- Protect the live site.
- Work only on a development branch until explicit sign-off.
- Mobile first, then expand for larger screens.
- Design the journey before writing implementation code.
- Use evidence and reasoning rather than automatic agreement.
- Preserve what already works.
- Prefer simple, reliable solutions over technical theatre.
- Review in small, understandable stages.
- Use real customer and job evidence wherever possible.
- Keep the quote action clear throughout the site.

---

## 22. Agreed Strategic Summary

Mr Move should be positioned around four equal core services:

1. Local removals
2. UK–Spain and Spain–UK relocations
3. Secure storage in Catral
4. Crane and specialist lifting

The homepage should introduce all four quickly, demonstrate trust through real evidence, and place a short quote form directly in the hero. Dedicated landing pages should then sell each service properly.

The design should retain the black, white, red, and gold brand palette, use genuine Mr Move photographs, integrate Google reviews, and present selected Facebook activity through a controlled Latest Moves section.

The immediate priority is not a full rebuild. It is a safer, clearer, faster, mobile-first conversion experience that turns the existing strengths of the business into more qualified enquiries.
