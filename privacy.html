Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This policy explains how Mr Move handles personal information submitted through this website, by phone, email or WhatsApp when you ask about removals, storage, packing, house clearances or crane access.

Who we are

Mr Move provides removals, storage and related moving services on the Costa Blanca and for wider Spain, UK and European relocations.

What information we collect

When you request a quote, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Preferred move date
  • Property type and access details
  • Services requested, such as removals, storage, packing, crane service or house clearance
  • Any message or extra details you choose to provide

How we use your information

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, prepare a quote, arrange bookings, provide customer service and keep basic records connected to the work requested.

Lawful basis

We process enquiry information because it is necessary to respond to your request before entering into a service agreement, and because we have a legitimate interest in handling customer enquiries properly.

Third-party services

This website may use Vercel for hosting and Resend for sending enquiry emails from the contact form. These services process data only as needed to provide hosting, delivery and website functionality.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and booking information only for as long as needed to manage the enquiry, provide the service, handle follow-up questions and meet basic legal or accounting requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, subject to any legal obligations that require records to be kept.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact info@mrmove.es.
